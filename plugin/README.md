# Cyango

Cyango edits a story that is open in Cyango xr/studio. This plugin includes the editing guide and connects your AI app to the hosted Cyango MCP server at `https://mcp.cyango.com/mcp`.

The first time you use it, your AI app asks you to sign in with your Cyango account and to allow access. Then open a story in Cyango xr/studio and turn MCP on in Story Settings. The assistant can then change scenes, layout, models, and interactions in that open story.

Cyango xr/studio has to be open and connected before an edit can land. Saving stays a manual step in the editor.
